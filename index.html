05 / Questions

Common questions.

When are you launching?

Early access opens in roughly four weeks. Waitlist members get in first - about two weeks before public launch.

Do I need to install an SDK or change my code?

No. SpendOps pulls aggregate cost and usage data from your providers' Admin APIs on a schedule. No SDK, no proxy, no code changes, and zero added latency - we're never in your request path, so an outage on our side can't touch your product.

What happens to my API key? Can you see our prompts?

You connect a read-only Admin key - not your production key. It's encrypted with AES-256 before it's stored, never returned to a browser, and never logged. The Admin APIs return token counts and dollar amounts, not content: we never see prompts, completions, or your users' data. Revoke the key at any time. SOC 2 is on our roadmap as we move upmarket.

Can it stop runaway spend, or just report it?

At launch: alerts at 80% and 100% of every budget you set, in Slack and email the same day - so a runaway loop costs you hours, not a month. Hard caps that auto-pause a key at its limit are on the roadmap.

Which providers do you support?

OpenAI and Anthropic at launch, normalized to USD in one view. Gemini cost ingestion is next on the roadmap, with Azure OpenAI and Bedrock to follow. Tell us your stack in the form above and we'll prioritize it.

What will it cost?

Plans start at $299/month, and every plan includes everything - attribution, anomaly alerts, budgets, Slack, and the monthly CFO report. Tiers scale with the spend we track for you. There's a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and waitlist members get founding-member perks at launch.

How is this different from Helicone, Langfuse, or Vantage?

Observability tools like Helicone and Langfuse are built for engineers debugging prompts and traces. Cloud-cost platforms like Vantage are built and priced for enterprises. SpendOps is finance-first at startup pricing: per-feature and per-customer dollar attribution, budgets, and a board-ready monthly report - the answers your CFO actually asks for.
